# Project-Olive-Drab
An original, real-time, roleplaying wargame.
## Summary
- Real Time
- Europe Focused
	- Expansions for other areas of the world
- Present Day
- WW3 Scenario
	- NATO vs Russia
- Multiple Players
- Different Playstyles Available
	- Civilian Government Roles
		- Head of State
			- Can take actions to influence the outcome of the war (economic, diplomatic, etc)
			- gives highest ranking military player orders, which they have to figure out. Military players cannot act without permission of Civilian player.
	- Military Roles
		- Corps Commander (Only on the largest of maps, like a Europe or Asia map)
			- Army Commander (Default Highest Rank)
				- Division Commander (Base Rank)
					- Brigade Commander (only on the smallest of maps, like a Syria or Ukraine map)
