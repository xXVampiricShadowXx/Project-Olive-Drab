# Design Notes

Use this file for ideas that are not ready to become rules yet. Keeping them here prevents unfinished ideas from being mistaken for decisions.

## How to record an idea

For each idea, include:

- **Idea:** What is the proposed rule or feature?
- **Purpose:** What player experience should it create?
- **Risk:** What could go wrong?
- **Test:** What small playtest could evaluate it?
- **Status:** Open, testing, accepted, or rejected.

## Ideas

### Example entry

- **Idea:** Orders may arrive with a delay based on distance and communication quality.
- **Purpose:** Make command structure and communication meaningful.
- **Risk:** Delays may feel arbitrary or create too much bookkeeping.
- **Test:** Use three simple delay cards in a one-hour scenario.
- **Status:** Open.
