# 2. Consent and safety

Read before role assignment and again before the first order. Olive Drab is a
fictionalized, supervised wargame, not a statement about real nations, current
events, military capability, or real-world operations.

**This learning page adds no rules.** It preserves the participant briefing;
linked governing sections control the rules it summarizes.

## What participation means

Participants may be NATO commander, Russia commander, GM, optional Observer,
or a temporary same-side commander where that role applies. The GM maintains
the authoritative record and may pause, suspend, place play on hiatus, adjust,
or end play at discretion, consulting players where practical and preserving
the game state. See
[established briefing rules](../templates/scenario-briefing.md#established-rules-do-not-rewrite-at-briefing).

Observers only watch and document in the playtest. They do not direct a side
or post unless a commander or GM interacts with them. There are no Observers
in the final actual game. See [Observer route](../templates/observer-guide.md).

Roleplay may shape fictional motivation, communication, or relationships,
never rules, hidden information, resolution, or consent. It is optional:
decline a prompt, fade to black, summarize, or request a neutral/lower-intensity
presentation. Ask for an accessibility adjustment, break, or clarification
at any time. Sources:
[roleplay boundaries](../reference/prototype/roleplay.md#information-and-roleplay-boundaries),
[safety](../reference/prototype/roleplay.md#safety-and-consent-expectations).

## Consent and real-life priority

- Participation is voluntary. Pause, take a break, withdraw, ask for a change,
  or ask the GM to stop **without giving a personal reason**.
- Real life comes first: safety, illness, emergencies, work, caregiving,
  accessibility, or private obligations are enough reason to pause or leave.
- Use the agreed pause/hiatus signal or the safest available contact with the
  GM. If you cannot explain, the GM preserves state and uses the recorded fallback.
- The GM records only the game consequence, such as `commander unavailable`,
  not personal circumstances. Personal details do not belong in an Observer
  ledger or public report.
- The GM discusses readiness with players, then decides when to resume.
  Pausing is not failure, needs no apology, and must not create an in-game
  advantage or disadvantage.

See [safety and consent expectations](../reference/prototype/roleplay.md#safety-and-consent-expectations).

## Information boundaries

Use game channels and the GM-visible record for orders, reports, rulings,
notifications, and map updates. Use a display or role name. Do not share:

- another side's private reports, hidden map, or GM-only conditions;
- personal contact details, identifying or health information;
- real-world operational information, political claims, or sensitive history;
- screenshots or transcripts outside the agreed participant group without
  removing private and identifying information.

Personal direct messages are not the game record. Keep your knowledge separate
from your character's: "My commander does not know that."
The GM gives an eligible temporary same-side successor only the absent role's
current orders, status, earned reports, and pending decisions, never opposing
private information. Temporary command does not apply in the one-commander-per-side
test; see [absence rules](../reference/prototype/operating-procedure.md#temporary-command-and-player-absence)
and [information boundaries](../reference/prototype/roleplay.md#information-and-roleplay-boundaries).

## Before starting

Know your role, channel path, notification method, pause/hiatus signal, and
how to contact the GM privately. The first test is **Day 1, 08:00-22:00**,
closing after the final control-state check; the broader seven-day clock
and overnight freeze are not this session's schedule.
See [clock](../reference/prototype/operating-procedure.md#campaign-clock).

No personal details, medical information, identity, availability beyond the
handoff need, or reasons for a boundary are required.
The GM asks for this simple confirmation:

```text
I understand the fictional scope, information boundaries, pause/withdrawal
option, and real-life priority rule. I know how to contact the GM privately.
```

It is **not a waiver** and must not include personal information.
