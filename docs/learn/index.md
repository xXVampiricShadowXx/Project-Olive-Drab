# Learn to play

New to Olive Drab? Start here for the first supervised human test.
These plain-language pages add no rules: **linked governing sections win**
if a summary differs. Human-playtest validation remains pending; no human
test has been completed.

## Required public reading for commanders

Read these three complete pages, in order:

1. [Essentials](essentials.md): what you play, the clock, and communication.
2. [Consent and safety](consent-and-safety.md): boundaries, breaks, and privacy.
3. [Commander guide](commanders.md): orders, map, contact, and the result.

**STOP: that is the end of the required public learning corpus.** Links to
governing sections are for checking a rule, not additional cover-to-cover
assignments. GM setup, administration, history, and decisions are not required
player reading.

Before play you must still receive and acknowledge your **private role sheet
and briefing from the GM**, propose starting placement for GM approval, and
participate in the communication rehearsal. These operational handoffs are
separate from public reading; never put filled private material in the repository.
The [commander checklist](commanders.md#commander-before-you-start-checklist)
collects the remaining preparation.

Keep the [quick reference](../reference/quick-reference.md) and
[glossary](../reference/glossary.md) available as optional live lookups.

## Other roles

| Role | Route |
|---|---|
| GM | [GM preparation](../gm/index.md): prepare, set up and rehearse, then final go/no-go |
| Observer (optional) | [Essentials](essentials.md), [consent and safety](consent-and-safety.md), then the [Observer guide](../templates/observer-guide.md) and [event ledger](../templates/observer-event-ledger.md) |

The repository supplies documentation, not a Discord server, shared map,
notification service, or roster. The GM must confirm external setup and
readiness before play. Browse the [documentation catalog](../index.md) only
when you need a source or another audience's materials.
