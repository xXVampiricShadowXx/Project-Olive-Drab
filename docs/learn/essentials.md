# 1. Essentials

Olive Drab is a fictionalized, human-moderated wargame: you make command
decisions, send written orders, and receive reports. The **game master (GM)**
is the neutral person who keeps the authoritative record and resolves uncertain
or contested actions. This first test checks communication, decisions, timing,
and that record, not real nations, current events, military capability, or
real-world operations.

**This is a non-authoritative learning summary, not new rules.** The linked
governing sections win if a summary differs. You need not read every linked
source cover-to-cover. Human-playtest validation is pending.

## What you will play

One NATO commander and one Russia commander each direct an infantry
**company**, represented by **1-4 echelons**. An echelon is an immediate
subordinate unit; each has one **force marker** on the map. The count is
recorded at setup and stays fixed. One neutral GM supervises. An optional
Observer watches and documents; they are not a required fourth participant.
See [scenario forces](../reference/prototype/scenario.md#initial-sides) and
[first-test roles](../reference/prototype/roles-and-command.md#first-prototype-roles).

The fictional town is **Brackenford**. Your objective is to secure and hold it
through the final Day 1 check under the
[published control rule](../reference/prototype/control-and-victory.md#primary-result-for-the-first-human-test).
This is an infantry-only test, not a full combat simulation: no vehicles,
artillery, air support, cyberwarfare, national politics, or detailed equipment.
It is preparation, not a release or a balance verdict.
See [packet boundaries](../reference/prototype/index.md#packet-boundaries).

## When you play

**Day 1, 08:00-22:00**, in the shared local time zone. One real minute equals
one scenario-clock minute; no calendar dates are fixed by the rules. You may
check in briefly or stay longer: the GM processes accepted orders while you
are away. At 22:00 the GM checks the final control state and closes the
session. Orders and response deadlines do not continue into Day 2.

The broader seven-day/multi-day procedure is for later tests or rehearsal,
not this session. Its overnight freeze pauses actions and timers, not the
advance of scenario time to 08:00.
Sources: [clock](../reference/prototype/operating-procedure.md#campaign-clock),
[flexible attention](../reference/prototype/operating-procedure.md#command-hierarchy-and-unit-terminology),
[closure](../reference/prototype/control-and-victory.md#end-of-scenario-procedure).

## Where you communicate

Join only the channels the GM provides. A Discord server, shared map,
notifications, and roster are external setup, not supplied by this repository;
treat them as unavailable until the GM confirms them.

| Route | Who and purpose |
|---|---|
| Group channel | Both commanders and GM: public updates, non-sensitive talk, rules procedure |
| Side-private channel | Your commander and GM: orders, reports, private status |
| Opposing contact | Contact with the other commander only with GM approval and visibility |

Use the designated game channel and required fields for orders and reports.
Personal direct messages are never orders, reports, rulings, map updates, or
notifications. See
[communication](../reference/prototype/operating-procedure.md#communication-and-chain-of-command).

## What to notice

Separate **what happened** from **what you think should change**: confusing
fields, missed notifications, unclear authority, timing friction, meaningful
or unmeaningful decisions, role gaps, accessibility or safety concerns.
Record findings through the [feedback framework](../project/feedback-and-revisions.md)
and [report template](../templates/playtest-report.md); remove names and personal
details before wider sharing. A balance verdict is not required.
