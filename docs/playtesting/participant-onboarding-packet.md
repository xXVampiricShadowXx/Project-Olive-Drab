# Participant Onboarding Packet

Welcome to a supervised Olive Drab prototype. The setting and factions are
fictionalized. This is a test of communication, command decisions, timing, and
the GM record—not a statement about real nations or current events.

## Before play

- Read the [prototype packet index](../game-design/09-prototype-packet-index.md).
- Join only the game channels provided by the GM.
- Use a display name or role name; do not share personal, health, political, or
  operational information.
- Ask for an accessibility adjustment, lower-intensity presentation, break, or
  clarification at any time.

## During play

Orders and reports go through the designated game channel and use the required
fields. A missing field may cause the GM to return an order before its timer
starts. Silence does not authorize an unlisted attack. The GM may pause,
adjust, or end play when real life requires it.

For the **first human test**, the active window is Day 1, 08:00–22:00 in the
shared local time zone, and the session closes after the Day 1 final control-state
check. The broader prototype can freeze overnight and resume at 08:00 when a
multi-day test actually continues; that broader procedure is not a continuation
of the first human session.

A same-side temporary commander may receive earned information during an
absence; opposing private information is not transferred.

## Consent and exit

Participation is voluntary. You may pause, take a break, withdraw without
explaining why, or ask the GM to stop. The GM records only the operational
consequence needed for continuity. Personal details do not belong in the
observer ledger or public report.

## What feedback is useful

Report what happened separately from what you think should change: confusing
fields, missed notifications, unclear authority, timing friction, meaningful
decisions, role gaps, accessibility concerns, or safety concerns. The first
test does not require a balance verdict.
