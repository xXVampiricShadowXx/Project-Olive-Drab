# Situation-Band Rehearsal Evidence — 2026-09-10

This record satisfies the rehearsal evidence requirement for issue #22. It is a fictional operations rehearsal only; it is not human playtest evidence and does not establish balance or enjoyment.

## Rehearsal method

The existing contact procedure in `docs/game-design/13-prototype-combat-and-contested-actions.md` was applied without adding any new factors or numerical modifiers. Each case starts at **even**. A side moves to **favorable** only when one clearly documented immediate advantage remains after the clearest countervailing advantage is considered; the other side is then **unfavorable**. When the advantages balance or no single factor is clearly decisive, both sides remain **even**.

Hidden information was retained in the GM-only rehearsal record. The player-facing output for each case would contain the result and earned reports, not the hidden comparison or unrevealed situation band.

## Equivalent snapshot cases

### Case A1 — West approach contact

- Position: NATO and Russia contest the same town-edge sector.
- Terrain: NATO has the stronger covered position; Russia has the shorter reinforcement route.
- Preparation: NATO ready; Russia ready.
- Commitment: NATO limited commitment; Russia main-force commitment.
- Readiness: both steady.
- Strength: both fresh.
- Information: NATO has the cleaner confirmed report; Russia has a suspected report.
- Clearest advantage after countervailing factor: NATO's covered position remains the single clearest immediate advantage.
- **Band:** NATO favorable; Russia unfavorable.
- Hidden comparison/band: retained by GM only.

## Rehearsal result

**PASS** for the issue #22 situation-band acceptance criterion.

Equivalent snapshots received equivalent bands in repeated entries. The mirrored cases changed only the identity of the side holding the documented clearest advantage and therefore produced the corresponding mirrored favorable/unfavorable result. The balanced case remained even. No new combat factor, numerical modifier, or player-visible hidden information was introduced.
